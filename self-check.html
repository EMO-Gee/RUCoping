Your Tally

Count how many times you selected "Yes":

  • 0 Yes: The experience may not meet common definitions of sexual assault. However, if you feel uncomfortable, support is still available.
  • 1–2 Yes: Some concerning elements may be present. Speaking to a counsellor may help clarify your experience.
  • 3 or more Yes: Your experience strongly aligns with common definitions of sexual assault. What happened is not your fault.

Next Steps
