Your Mental Health Matters

Understanding Mental Health

Mental health is a state of well-being in which an individual realizes their own potential, can cope with the normal stresses of life, can work productively and fruitfully, and is able to contribute to their community.

Health is state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity. Mental health is represents a state of well-being where a student recognise his or her own capabilities, handles life's normal pressures effectively, works productively and society. In essence, mental health is a crucial aspect of overall well-being and quality of life.

What is mental illness?

Mental illness
Mental illness refers to a wide range of mental health conditions that affect a person's mood, thinking, and behavior.

These conditions can include:

  • Depression
  • Anxiety disorders
  • Schizophrenia
  • Eating disorders
  • Addictive behaviors

If left untreated, they can make life seem unbearable and contribute to self-harm including suicide.
