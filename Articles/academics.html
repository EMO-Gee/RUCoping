Academic Resources

Tools and tips to help you succeed in your studies

Smart Study Strategies

Instead of cramming, try breaking your study sessions into focused 25-minute intervals with 5-minute breaks in between (Pomodoro Technique). This can help improve concentration and retention. Also, consider using active recall and spaced repetition techniques to enhance memory. Don't just re-read your notes - quiz yourself or teach the material to a friend to really solidify your understanding.

Time Management & Burnout

Struggling to balance your studies with social life and self-care? Remember, it's okay to say no to some social events if you need to focus on your work. Use a planner or digital calendar to keep track of your commitments, and make sure to set aside time for activities that help you relax and recharge.

Finding Campus Resources

Don't forget about the amazing resources available on campus! The library offers amazing services, and there are often workshops on time management and exam preparation. Check out the student services website for more information on how to access these resources.
